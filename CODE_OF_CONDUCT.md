# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be constructive,
assume good intent while evaluating technical claims on evidence, respect
privacy and security boundaries, and welcome contributors regardless of
experience or background.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal
attacks, deliberate disruption, publication of private information, and
security testing without authorization.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `lihuo0229@outlook.com`. Project
maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other
participation that violates these standards, and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation.

Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, prior behavior, and the
safety of affected people. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.
